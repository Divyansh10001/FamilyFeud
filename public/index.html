📜 Official Television Show Rules

HOW TO PLAY LIKE THE REAL SHOW

01

The Face-Off

One representative from each family meets at the buzzer. The first player to buzz in gives an answer. If they guess the #1 answer, their family wins control and chooses to PLAY or PASS!

02

Playing the Board

The playing family takes turns guessing remaining answers with NO conferring! Correct answers reveal tiles and accumulate points in the Bank. Wrong guesses earn a Strike (1, 2, 3).

03

The 3-Strike Steal

If the playing family strikes out 3 times, the defending family confers for ONE chance to steal. If their answer is on the board, they steal the ENTIRE Bank! Otherwise, original team keeps it.

04

300 Pts & Fast Money

Points multiply: 1X (Rounds 1-2), 2X (Round 3), 3X (Round 4+). First family to 300 points wins the match and moves to Fast Money (5 questions, 2 players, 20s/25s, 200 pts for $20,000)!
